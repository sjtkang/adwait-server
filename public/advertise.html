What you are buying

A viewable impression, defined precisely.

At least50% of the ad visible
For at least1 continuous second
In theactive browser tab
StandardIAB display viewability
Anything short of that is not billed, not counted, and not credited to the user either.

Most display inventory bills on served impressions and reports viewability afterwards. AdWait counts only the impressions that met the bar in the first place, because the same measurement decides what the person watching gets paid. The incentive to inflate does not exist: every fake impression would cost us a real payout.

  • Bring your own measurement. If your numbers and ours disagree, we reconcile to yours.
  • Minimum-viewable-time reporting is included, not an upsell.
  • Background tabs, scrolled-away cards and sub-second flashes are all excluded automatically.

Straight answers

The questions buyers actually ask.

How big is the audience right now?

AdWait is new, and we will tell you the real number when you ask rather than a range designed to impress. That is exactly why the entry pilot exists at $100: the correct way to evaluate a young network is a small prepaid test with honest reporting, not a media kit. If we cannot deliver your volume in a reasonable window, we will tell you before invoicing.

Is this brand safe?

Your ad appears on nine mainstream AI tools and nowhere else. There is no user-generated content around it, no comment section, and no adjacent inventory. One ad renders at a time, always labeled, always in an isolated container that cannot cover the page's own interface.

Do you track clicks or conversions?

No. A click opens your site in a new tab and we record nothing about it. Tag your destination URL and measure on your own analytics. We would rather report five numbers we can stand behind than fifteen we cannot.

Can I verify the impression counts?

Yes, and we would prefer you did. Run your own viewability measurement on the creative. Billing reconciles to verified counts, and if your numbers are lower than ours, yours win.

Is there a frequency cap?

Not currently, and that is deliberate on a network this size: repetition is how a small audience produces a readable result, and the person watching is paid for every impression. You get full reach and frequency reporting, so you can judge the tradeoff yourself and we will cap on request.

Who is behind this?

AdWait is built and run by Tommy Kang, a solo founder. You deal with him directly: the person who answers your email is the person who runs your campaign and writes your report. If you want the exact rules an impression has to pass before you are billed for it, ask and you will get them in writing.
