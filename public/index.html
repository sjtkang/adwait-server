How it works

How you get paid while you use ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini

Install it, then chat exactly the way you already do. The only thing that changes is who gets paid for the wait.

  1. Install from the Chrome Web Store. No account, no sign-up, no email. 01
  2. Chat as normal. While a response is generating, one small ad card appears in a corner you choose. 02
  3. Only real views count. An ad must be at least 50% visible, in your active tab, for at least one second — the IAB viewability standard — before it earns you anything. 03
  4. Longer waits, more ads. During long generations the card rotates every 7 seconds, and each genuinely viewed ad counts. 04
  5. Cash out at $5. Straight from the popup, to a PayPal email or USDC on Base or Polygon. 05

Trust

Anonymous by design. Honest by default.

No account, ever.

Earnings accrue to an anonymous per-install ID. The only identity Adwait ever asks for is a payout destination — and only at the moment you cash out.

Recoverable without a sign-up.

An earnings key lets you back up your balance and restore it later. Recovery, minus the account.

Every ad says it's an ad.

Each one is labeled "Ad · Adwait". Clicks open in a new tab and are never tracked.

Estimates that admit it.

Displayed earnings are honest estimates until reconciled against advertiser-side verification — about a 30-day review before payout.

Nothing fake, ever.

If no paid campaign is live, the card says "Out of ads right now." You'll see the truth before you ever see filler.
