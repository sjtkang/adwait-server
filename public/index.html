Payments

Where the money comes from.

The math, in the open

Advertisers pay per 1,000 viewed ads (a “CPM”). You keep half. A $4 CPM campaign pays you $0.002 per view — small on its own, and it adds up quietly while you do what you were doing anyway.

Honest numbers

Your balance is an estimate until it’s reconciled against advertiser-verified counts — that’s why payouts have a review window instead of being instant. If no campaign is live, the card says so and nothing accrues. Earnings only ever come from real, paid campaigns.

Cashing out

  • From $5, straight from the extension popup.
  • PayPal email or USDC (Base / Polygon) — you choose at cash-out, and that’s the only moment any contact detail is asked for.
  • Requests are reviewed and paid within about 30 days. Fees may apply depending on the method.
  • Your anonymous earnings key restores your balance on a new install — still no account.

Privacy

What we don’t collect.

  • Your conversations. Adwait never reads, stores, or transmits your prompts or the AI’s responses — it only checks whether the page’s own “stop generating” control is visible.
  • Your identity. No name, no email, no sign-in. A random install ID is all that exists — until you cash out, when you type only a payout destination.
  • Your browsing. The extension runs on nine supported AI sites and nowhere else, and you can switch each one off.
  • Per ad, three things are recorded: which ad, which supported site, and how long it was actually on screen.
  • No cookies. No ad clicks tracked. Clicking an ad simply opens the advertiser’s site.
  • Uninstall ends everything — local data is deleted and collection stops immediately.

FAQ

Fair questions.

How much will I actually earn?

It depends on how much you use AI and which campaigns are live. As a shape: a heavy user seeing ~40 ads a day at a $4 CPM earns about $2.40 a month; premium campaigns pay more. This is pocket money for time you were already spending — not an income program, and we won’t pretend otherwise.

Do you read my chats?

No. Detection works by noticing the page’s own stop-generating button — an element on the page, not your words. Conversation text is never read or transmitted, and the privacy policy is written to be checkable against exactly that behavior.

Which sites are supported?

ChatGPT, Claude (including Claude Code and Claude Design on the web), Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, DeepSeek, Microsoft Copilot, Replit, and Base44 — nine sites, each with its own on/off switch. More AI surfaces, especially long-running agents, are on the roadmap.

What if there are no ads to show?

The card says “Out of ads right now” and nothing is recorded or credited. Your balance only grows from real, funded campaigns.

How do payouts work?

From $5, request a payout in the popup with a PayPal email or a USDC address (Base or Polygon). Requests are reviewed against advertiser-verified counts and paid within about 30 days; provider or network fees may apply. Multiple requests can be in review at once.

What’s the earnings key?

Your balance lives against a random key in your browser instead of an account. Copy it from the popup and keep it somewhere safe: pasting it into a fresh install restores your balance on a new machine. Treat it like a password — anyone holding it can cash out that balance.
