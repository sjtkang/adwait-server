2What AdWait is

AdWait is a free Chrome extension that shows one clearly labeled ad in a corner of supported AI websites while a response is generating, and credits you 50% of the advertising revenue for every ad you genuinely see. A view counts when the ad is at least 50% visible, in the active tab, for at least one continuous second. When no advertiser campaign is live, the card says so and nothing accrues.

4Earnings and balances

The balance in the extension is an estimate until it is reconciled against advertiser-verified impression counts. Reconciliation happens at payout review and can adjust a balance. The value of a view depends on which campaigns are live at that moment; there is no fixed rate, no minimum earnings, and no guarantee that ads will be available at any given time.

AdWait is pocket money for time you were already spending. It is not an income program, and nothing on our pages should be read as a promise of earnings.

5Your earnings key

Your identity in AdWait is a random install ID, shown in the popup as your earnings key. It is a bearer credential: anyone who has it can direct the balance attached to it. Keep it private. If you lose it, the balance attached to it cannot be recovered, and we cannot verify ownership claims for a key we never linked to a person.

6Cash-outs

  • The minimum payout is $5.00. A claim requests your full available balance.
  • You choose a destination when you claim: an email address (delivered as a Tremendous reward link, redeemable as PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, US bank transfer, or a virtual Visa card) or a USDC wallet address on the Base network. Available methods can vary by region.
  • For email payouts, the reward provider's delivery fee is deducted from your claimed balance. For USDC, blockchain network conditions may affect the amount received.
  • Every claim is reviewed by a person, and payment typically arrives within about 3 days of the claim.
  • Cryptocurrency transfers are final and irreversible. If you provide a wrong or incompatible address, the funds cannot be recovered. USDC is sent on the Base network only.
  • Taxes on your earnings are your responsibility. Where the law requires it, we may ask for tax information (for example a W-9 or W-8 form) before paying, and may report payments to tax authorities.
